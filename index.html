    What you're watching

    A 34-second, 1,020-frame (30 fps) explainer rendered entirely in code. Every frame is a pure function of its index, so scrubbing, stepping, and exported video all show the same frame.

    It traces one signing request through a hardware security module, defined by NIST as a physical computing device that safeguards and manages cryptographic keys and provides cryptographic processing. The walkthrough covers key generation, authorization, signing inside the boundary, verification outside it, and the audit trail.

    Scope and honesty

    • Conceptual schematic. Layout, sizes, and materials are illustrative, not a product geometry.
    • Non-exportability of key K1 is an explicitly selected configuration.
    • No performance figures, certifications, or vendor internals are claimed.
    • Time is compressed. Energy-to-heat is shown qualitatively, not measured.
    • Camera, palette, and timing are original illustrative choices.
